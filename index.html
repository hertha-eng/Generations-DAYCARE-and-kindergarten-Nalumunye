School Facts

Key details parents and search tools can verify quickly.

This page summarizes the main facts families often need when comparing daycare and kindergarten options in Kampala.

Location and access

Generations Daycare and Kindergarten is located at 7GCC+QG, Kampala, Uganda, serving families in Kampala and nearby communities who want a local preschool option.

Programs and ages

The school supports children from 6 months to 6 years through daycare, kindergarten foundations, and kindergarten readiness stages.

Ways to contact

Parents can call 0779399033, email info@generationselementary.org, chat on WhatsApp, or use the contact and admissions pages to ask questions and book a visit.

Programs

Three stages of early growth.

Each stage is explained in a simple way so parents can understand what fits their child best and what to expect next.

6 months - 2 years

Daycare

Comfort, routine, sensory play, and careful supervision for infants and toddlers.

  • Warm daily care and safe transitions
  • Language-rich interaction
  • Movement, music, and early social development
3 - 6 years

Kindergarten

School-readiness support with engaging foundations for primary success.

  • Reading, counting, and guided thinking
  • Classroom independence and confidence
  • Structured preparation for the next stage
