Admissions

A clear path from first inquiry to enrollment.

Families searching for admissions in Kampala need clear next steps. This page explains the process, age groups, documents, and contact options in one helpful place.

1. Make an inquiry

Call, WhatsApp, or visit the school to discuss the right age group, availability, and your child’s needs.

2. Complete admissions details

Share child and parent information so the school can guide you toward the best program and next steps.

3. Confirm enrollment

Submit documents, review the final details, and prepare for a smooth and confident school start.

Required documents

  • Copy of the child’s birth certificate
  • Recent passport photo
  • Parent or guardian contact details
  • Completed admissions information

Age groups

  • Daycare: 6 months - 2 years
  • Kindergarten Foundations: 3 - 4 years
  • Kindergarten Readiness: 5 - 6 years
Helpful Before You Apply

Questions parents often ask before starting admissions.

Clear answers make enrollment feel easier, especially for parents comparing different schools nearby.

Can I ask questions first?

Yes. Many parents contact the school by phone or WhatsApp before completing any form, especially to ask about availability and visits.

Can I visit the school?

Yes. A school visit is a helpful way to see the environment, ask questions, and feel more confident before enrolling.

What makes the process easier?

Having your child’s age, basic details, and preferred program ready helps the school guide you more quickly.

Admissions Facts

Useful details for parents who want the process explained clearly.

This page is structured to answer common admissions questions directly, which also gives AI search systems a stronger page to reference for enrollment information.

Who can apply

Parents can apply for children aged 6 months to 6 years across daycare, kindergarten foundations, and kindergarten readiness stages.

What to prepare

Families are usually asked for the child’s birth certificate copy, a passport photo, parent or guardian contact details, and admissions information.

What happens next

After the first inquiry, the school can help with program fit, school visits, required details, and the final steps needed to confirm enrollment.

Before You Send

Parents get the quickest help when they include a few key details in their message.

  • Your child’s age and preferred program
  • When you hope your child can start
  • Whether you want to schedule a school visit
  • Any question about availability, fees, or daily routine
