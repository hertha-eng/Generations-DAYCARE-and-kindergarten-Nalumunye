Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Generations Daycare and Kindergarten may collect, use, and protect information shared through this website, phone calls, email, WhatsApp, or admissions inquiries.

Last updated: April 24, 2026

Information We May Collect

We may collect information that you choose to provide when you contact us or complete an inquiry form.

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Child's name, age, and preferred program
  • Any message, visit request, or admissions question you send us

How We Use Information

Information you provide may be used to respond to your inquiry, share admissions details, arrange a school visit, or communicate about services offered by the school.

We do not request unnecessary personal information through this website.

How We Protect Information

We aim to keep information shared with the school handled responsibly and only for school-related communication. However, no website or online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information provided through this website. Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary for school communication, lawful obligations, or service support connected to school inquiries.

Children's Information

This website is intended for parents, guardians, and adults making school inquiries. If information about a child is shared with us, it should only be provided by a parent or legal guardian for admissions or school-related purposes.

Contact About Privacy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to ask about information you have submitted, please contact Generations Daycare and Kindergarten through the contact details provided on this website.
